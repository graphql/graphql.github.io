# GraphQL Ambassador Program 

Ambassadors are trusted go-to community leaders driven by a passion for GraphQL projects and strive to nurture the growing GraphQL community through collaboration, education and outreach.

GraphQL Ambassadors are not necessarily required to be deep GraphQL experts (although it's definitely appreciated), but they need to share a passion for helping others and spreading the GraphQL word and recognized best practices.
