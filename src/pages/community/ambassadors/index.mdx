## Ambassador Emeriti

We want to celebrate and thank these folks for everything they have brought to the program and to the wider GraphQL community. Our Ambassador Emeriti will always be an important part of the story of the GraphQL Ambassador Program. By stepping forward, sharing their time and experience, and lending their support to a new community initiative, they helped give the program its start and its sense of possibility. We're incredibly grateful for the part they have played, and proud to recognize them as part of the community that continues to make GraphQL stronger, more welcoming, and more connected.
