In April, we introduced [the first GraphQL ecosystem Call For Projects](https://graphql.org/blog/2026-04-13-call-for-projects/)!

This Call for Projects resulted in [three exciting proposals](https://graphql.org/blog/2026-06-16-announcing-h1-2026-graphql-community-grants/): 

* GraphiQL 6 ([blog post](https://graphql.org/blog/2026-11-07-introducing-graphiql-6/))
* Interactive tutorials
* GraphQL.js maintenance grant

As 2026 went by, it's been hard to find new grant topics though. Finding new projects requires a lot of iteration on both the scope and stipend. Those discussions have been hard to finalize.

There is no shortage of work though! 

## 🧑‍💻 Maintenance grants

In order to lower friction, we're trying something new in 2027 with "maintenance grants". 

Maintenance grants have a fixed stipend and scope:

* Stipend: $3k, fixed for all projects.
* Scope: single git repository: triaging issues and pull requests, making releases, patching security issues, and more.

We expect maintainers to at least triage issues and make releases. If a repo has multiple maintainers, the stipend can be split equally between them.

We realize the stipend is typically not enough to fund what's typically involved in maintaining and evolving a large repository. The stipend goal is not to fund projects 100%, but it is a contribution to help projects become more sustainable and show appreciation for the people moving GraphQL. 

This is also just the beginning. We are hoping to grow the grant program. If your company or someone you know wants to help, please ask them to [join the foundation](https://graphql.org/community/foundation/join/) or contribute to [the crowdfund](https://fund.graphql.org) so we can improve funding for critical GraphQL projects.

In addition to the stipend, selected projects will be promoted on foundation channels: social networks, mentions at GraphQLConf, etc.

At the end of 2027, we will also write a report of impact of the program with all grantees.

## ❓ Questions

Q: what happens to the previous grant program? Can I still submit a grant proposal if I have another project I want to fund?

We have limited resources and won't be able to fund a lot of projects outside maintenance grants. Things may evolve though and we want to help however we can. If you need help with a project, please reach out publicly on [Discord](https://discord.graphql.org), channel `#wg` or privately by email at [operations@graphql.org](mailto:operations@graphql.org)

Q: can I submit as an organization?

No. The grants are only attributed to individuals. If your project has multiple maintainers, apply once with all other maintainers as a comment and we will split the stipend between the different maintainers.

Q: what is the administrative process? 

You can read more about the process in the [handbook](https://handbooks.graphql.org/grants/faq#how-does-the-payment-process-work). Grants require signature of a grant agreement ([preview](https://github.com/graphql/foundation/blob/HEAD/grants/GraphQL_Grantee_Agreement-preview.pdf)).

Q: what projects are eligible?

Projects need to be open source and benefit the community. We typically encourage grants for the [foundation projects](https://github.com/orgs/graphql/repositories) but other projects will also be considered.
